Plumbing on Bonita Beach

If you own a place on the island, you already know how fast a leak turns into a bigger problem when the house is empty or a storm is on the way. Getting a call returned quickly matters more here than most places.

Bonita Beach sits on Little Hickory Island, a barrier island between the Gulf of Mexico and Estero Bay that is reached by road along Bonita Beach Road and Hickory Boulevard. It is part of the City of Bonita Springs. The island has public beach accesses, including the small Bonita Beach Park with its boardwalk. Public real-estate listing data counts roughly 1,700 homes here, with a median build year around 1980, spread across beachfront and bayfront houses, condominiums and townhouses. Those numbers are approximate.

What Makes the Island Different

Hurricane season runs from June 1 to November 30, and listing sources note that Hurricane Ian in 2022 changed the island significantly. The City of Bonita Springs encourages residents to keep a hurricane plan and sign up for emergency alerts. For plumbing, the practical points are below.

Water after a storm

After flooding or heavy rain, standing water can reach water heaters, valves and low-lying lines. If water has entered the home, see our water damage restoration page for what to do first.

Vacation and seasonal homes

A rental or part-year home may sit unused for weeks. Closing the main valve between stays and having someone check the property can limit the damage from a hidden leak.

Condo rules and shared lines

In a condo building, some risers and drains are shared. Your association may have rules for who can work on them and when, so contact the manager before a repair starts.

Services for Island Homes and Condos

These are general services that suit a coastal setting. They aren't a claim about any particular property.

  • Leak detection for hidden leaks, including in vacant homes
  • Burst pipe repair when a line fails and water needs to be stopped fast
  • Water heater repair and replacement
  • Water damage restoration after a leak or storm water
  • General plumbing repair for fixtures, valves and supply lines

Salt Air and Plumbing Fixtures

Salt in the air can speed up corrosion on exposed metal, such as outdoor fittings, hose bibs and fixtures near the water. It isn't a reason to replace anything by itself, but it is a good reason to look over exposed parts each year and fix small drips before they spread.

Before a Storm

  • Know where your main water shutoff is, and how to close it.
  • If you leave for the season, close the main valve and have someone check on the property.
  • Take photos of your water heater and plumbing fixtures for insurance records.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Bonita Beach part of the City of Bonita Springs?

Yes. Bonita Beach, on Little Hickory Island, is within the City of Bonita Springs.

What should I do first if water gets into my beach home?

Stay safe, shut off the main water valve if it's reachable and safe to do so, and avoid electrical hazards. Then call a plumber for any broken lines and a water damage professional to dry out the home.

Can I get plumbing work done while my condo is vacant?

Often yes, but you'll want to arrange access with your property manager or association first. Tell us when you call who can let a technician in.
