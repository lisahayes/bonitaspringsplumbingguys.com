Plumbing Service Areas Near Bonita Springs, FL

We cover the City of Bonita Springs plus the nearby SWFL communities homeowners here move between most, from Fort Myers Beach down through Naples.

Bonita Springs Communities

These five sit inside Bonita Springs city limits. Each has its own housing age, water source, and typical plumbing issue.

Bonita Bay

A golf community with a gate. It runs newer PEX pipe, on BSU water and sewer.

Learn More →

Pelican Landing

A large gated community with mixed home ages. Mostly on BSU-supplied water, with hard-water scale a common issue.

Learn More →

The Brooks

A planned area that includes Shadow Wood, Spring Run, and Copperleaf. Mostly on newer PEX pipe.

Learn More →

Imperial Shores

An older, river-adjacent neighborhood with copper supply lines. Some homes are still on private well and septic.

Learn More →

Village Walk of Bonita Springs

A gated community of single homes and villas. Home ages and setups here are fairly alike.

Learn More →

Nearby SWFL Cities We Serve

We also cover the towns and areas nearby. Roughly a fifteen-mile drive.

Estero, FL

A fast-growing city just north of Bonita Springs. A mix of established and newly built neighborhoods.

Learn More →

Naples, FL

Coastal homes and condos. Salt-air corrosion and hard water are the most common plumbing complaints.

Learn More →

East Naples, FL

A mix of older established neighborhoods and newer infill construction. South of downtown Naples.

Learn More →

Naples Park, FL

A dense grid of small coastal lots. Many still have older plumbing that hasn't been updated since original construction.

Learn More →

Pelican Bay, FL

An upscale coastal area. A mix of tall condo buildings and single homes on city water.

Learn More →

San Carlos Park, FL

An established area with older housing stock. A real mix of plumbing ages and materials.

Learn More →

Golden Gate, FL

An inland Naples-area community. Well and septic systems are still common on larger estate lots.

Learn More →

Fort Myers Beach, FL

An island community, rebuilding after Hurricane Ian. Heavy salt air and storm surge here.

Learn More →

Fort Myers, FL

Lee County's seat, spanning historic downtown homes to newer construction near Gateway.

Learn More →

North Naples, FL

Gated golf communities along Livingston Road, closer to Bonita Springs than central Naples.
