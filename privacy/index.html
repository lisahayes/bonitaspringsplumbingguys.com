Privacy Policy

Last updated August 2026. This page explains what information we collect and how it's used.

Information We Collect

When you call or otherwise contact Bonita Springs Plumbing Guys, we may collect basic information needed to provide service. That includes your name, phone number, service address, and details about the plumbing issue you're contacting us about. We do not use online contact forms. Scheduling happens by phone.

How We Use Information

Information you provide is used to schedule service, dispatch a technician, follow up on completed work, and keep basic business records. We do not sell customer information to third parties.

Website Data

Like most websites, this site may use standard analytics tools to understand general traffic patterns. Things like which pages get visited and how people arrive at the site. This data is grouped together and isn't used to identify individual visitors.

Cookies

This site may use basic cookies needed for standard website function and analytics. You can turn off cookies through your browser settings. Doing so may affect some site features.

Third-Party Links

This site may link to third-party resources, such as Google Maps. We aren't responsible for the privacy practices of those outside sites.

Contact Us

Questions about this privacy policy can be directed to us by phone at (239) 522-0887. Or by mail at 24301 Production Cir, Bonita Springs, FL 34135.

Changes to This Policy
